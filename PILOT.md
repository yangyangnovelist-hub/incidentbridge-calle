# Join the IncidentBridge pilot

IncidentBridge separates a consented synthetic CALL-E check from a real incident-operator workflow
pilot. Neither path uses a random vendor number or production incident data.

## Track A — consented transport validation

Any adult in a CALL-E-supported calling region may volunteer for one short, AI-disclosed role-play
using the fixed ExampleCloud incident in [`LIVE-SUCCESS-DEMO.md`](LIVE-SUCCESS-DEMO.md). The
recipient gets the expected facts and ticket before the call. This can prove real transport,
corroboration and `vendor_acknowledged` routing while `incident_closed` stays `false`; it is not a
real vendor deployment.

## Track B — incident-operator workflow pilot

An SRE, on-call engineer or incident commander can run a preview-only tabletop with a sanitized
incident brief. The pilot measures approval and review minutes, interruptions avoided, evidence
usefulness and false-success handling. No production secret, customer identifier or real support
credential is required, and the tabletop cannot place a phone call.

## Privacy and consent

A participant may stop at any time. Public proof excludes the phone number, participant identity,
transcript and recording. A real-provider artifact contains only the call ID, terminal status,
redacted structured facts, binding decision and explicit synthetic-scenario boundary.

To volunteer, use **Message manager** on the
[IncidentBridge Devpost page](https://devpost.com/software/incidentbridge-3sohvn). Do not post a
phone number in a public issue or discussion. The exact scenario and publication boundary will be
shared first.
